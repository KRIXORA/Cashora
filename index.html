How to use Cashora
1
Dashboard

Balance, monthly income/expense, cash-flow chart, budget gauge, and safe-to-spend. Sample data loads automatically the first time so every widget is live.

2
Add money movement

Tap + Add Transaction or press N. Mark “Repeat” for salary/rent. Search with /.

3
Budgets & Goals

Set category limits and savings targets. Insights and the bell icon warn when you cross 85% / 100%.

4
Net Worth

Track assets, debts (avalanche/snowball), 50/30/20 rule, and upcoming bills from recurring rules.

5
Analytics & Settings

Month comparison, subscription detection, CSV import/export, JSON backup, theme toggle, and reset/sample controls.

Not financial advice. Demo numbers are examples only — replace them with your real data anytime from Settings.
